# goMarkableStream

Stream your reMarkable tablet screen to any device with full color support and exceptional efficiency.

## Core Features

### 🎨 Full Color Streaming
Full color support for reMarkable 2 (firmware 3.24+) and Paper Pro.

### 🔋 Preserve Battery Life
- Auto-sleep after 2 seconds of inactivity, instant wake on writing
- 95-99% bandwidth reduction through delta streaming

### 📱 Stream Anywhere
Works seamlessly over VPN, mobile data, and Tailscale. Share publicly via Tailscale Funnel.

### 🎯 Presentation Tools
- Laser pointer (press `L`)
- Instant rotation (press `R`)
- Reveal.js slide control with swipe gestures
- Layer control for drawings
- Fullscreen mode

### 💎 Modern Interface
Glassmorphism dark theme with mobile-responsive design. Real-time connection status and auto-reconnection.

### 🔐 Security
HTTPS encryption, Tailscale VPN support, basic authentication. No cloud services - your data stays private.

## Use Cases

Perfect for presentations, teaching, remote collaboration, content creation, and productivity.

## Performance

Tested and proven on mobile tethering, VPN tunneling, and full color PDFs.
