Checking…

Anyone with the viewer link can see your entire displayed screen while sharing. Sleep, power, Stop, or the timer ends sharing. Waking never restarts it. Automatic rotation is not enabled.

Stop sharing keeps this panel reachable. Power down turns off the sharing server and its Tailscale connection—not the tablet or Daily Board.

Both viewing and control links will go offline. To bring them back, restart the tablet using its normal Restart action. Simply waking it is not enough. Capture will remain off after restart.
